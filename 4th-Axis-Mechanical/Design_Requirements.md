### Resolution
Very good 3D printer resolution is ~0.05 mm. We want to achieve this linear resolution at the largest diameter printable on the mandrels. In their current configuration, this is a diameter of 20 mm. Thus, the minimum angular resolution is:
0.286° or 0.005 rad = 0.05 [mm] / 10 [mm]

### Speed
We want a reasonable printing speed of ~60 mm/s at the surface of the mandrel. This is an angular speed of:
180.46 rpm or 18.898 rad/s = 60 [mm/s] / 6.35 [mm]

### Repeatability
We want a linear repeatability within our resolution, so same angular value of 0.286° ideally
